## 📊 Understanding the Metrics

The program shows several numbers. Here's what they mean in plain English:

- **Reaction Time** – How fast the AI notices an obstacle (in milliseconds). Lower is better.
- **Jump Accuracy** – Percentage of jumps that successfully cleared an obstacle. Higher is better.
- **Crash Count** – How many times the AI hit something. You want this to stay at zero.
- **Speed Adaptation** – How quickly the AI adjusts when the game speeds up.

These metrics help you understand which AI is better at handling different situations.
